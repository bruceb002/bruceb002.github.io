About Me

Hi, I'm Bruce Bai! I'm an aspiring software engineer currently completing my Master’s degree in Computer Science and Data Science at the University of Southern California (USC), following my undergraduate studies at UC Santa Cruz.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to build a robust professional foundation through four software engineering internships—spanning companies like Medtronic, Best Egg, and Marlette Funding LLC. My engineering work ranges from full-stack enterprise web development and migrating legacy systems to optimizing database queries and building C++ Proof of Concepts that dramatically cut load times.
